# Design rulings — 2026-08-08

Two open gear-check violations from the [full audit](audit/AUDIT_2026-08-08.md),
ruled using the procedure in `.claude/skills/rpg-game-design`: measure, distrust
the instrument, classify, rule with a number, guard it.

---

## Ruling 1 — Z10 Void Leviathan: ATK 53,333 → 64,000 (×1.20)

**Status: SHIPPED.** `combat_manager.gd`, `z10_boss_leviathan.stats.atk`.

### The finding

`boss_gearcheck` rule is *Common and Uncommon must LOSE, Rare and above must
WIN*. Z10 failed it — tier-matched Uncommon cleared the capstone **9/9 at
~263 s**.

### What the measurement said

Built `scripts/sim/z10_gate_diag.gd` to find which axis the gate turns on,
because that decides the lever:

| Rarity | Result (before) |
|---|---|
| Common | 0/9, **died** at 185 s, boss 40% left |
| Uncommon | 9/9, 263 s, **zero deaths in 9** |
| Rare | 9/9, 177 s |
| Legendary | 9/9, 143 s |

The gate turns on **survival, not damage**. Common dies; Uncommon never does.
That immediately rules out the obvious fix: Uncommon does not time out, so
raising HP cannot stop it. Enough HP to push Uncommon past the 1500 s cap
(~5.7×) would drag Rare to ~1000 s and Legendary to ~815 s — three times longer
than a boss should run.

An ATK sweep showed Rare is **unmoved across the entire band** — 176 s at ×1.0,
188 s at ×2.5, which is noise. Uncommon is a knife-edge: 9/9 in one run and 3/5
in another at ×1.0, then 0/5 by ×1.3. So there is a wide safe corridor to spend
and only Uncommon is sensitive to it.

First attempt at ×1.40 overshot: Uncommon died at 72 s with **76% boss HP left**.
That satisfies the rule but reads as "you are not welcome here" rather than
"you are nearly ready", and a gate with no near-miss row teaches nothing.

### The ruling

**×1.20.** Measured clean:

| Rarity | Result (after) |
|---|---|
| Common | 0/9, dies 105 s, boss 66% left |
| Uncommon | 0/9, dies 145 s, boss **46% left** |
| Rare | 9/9, **218 s** |
| Legendary | 9/9, **152 s** |

Uncommon now takes the boss past halfway before dying — visible progress,
obviously nearly possible, come back with one more tier. Rare and Legendary sit
inside the 90–300 s band a boss should occupy. There is 20% margin over the ×1.0
knife-edge, so the guard will not flip red on variance.

### Declined

- **Raising HP.** Wrong lever (Uncommon never times out) and it would wreck
  Rare/Legendary TTK.
- **Touching the rarity curve.** It is a shared constant that forty other
  encounters depend on; one boss's problem does not justify moving it.
- **×1.10**, which gave the prettiest near-miss (21% boss HP left) but sits only
  10% above the knife-edge — too thin for a stable guard.

### Guard

`scenes/boss_gearcheck.tscn` — Z10 now reports OK, and the suite is **11 of 15**
honoring the rule (was 10). `scenes/z10_gate_diag.tscn` is kept as a diagnostic
(no assertions; it reports the matrix) for the next question of this shape.

## Ruling 2 — Z12–Z15: the exotic weapon ladder is missing. Do not tune this.

**Status: CLOSED. Weapons + defence shipped; gear-check is 15/15, 0 violations.**

### The finding

All four NG+ Loop 1 bosses lose **0/9 at every rarity including Legendary**,
even with the correct mixed cryo+corrosion battery that their phase gate demands.

### Why this is a design gap, not a balance problem

The conventional ladder ships **30 weapons across 10 zones** — a fresh tier
every zone. The exotic channel ships **two weapons for five zones**:

| Module | atk_cryo | Era |
|---|---|---|
| `cryo_lance` | 10,000 | Z11 (first-warp unlock) |
| `corrosion_blaster` | 12,000 | Z11/Z12 |

Those two are the *only* things that breach a phase band. So across Z11 → Z15
the player's conventional power scales normally while their **exotic power is
frozen at tier 11** — and exotic power is the only kind that counts there.

Meanwhile boss HP keeps climbing:

| Zone | Effective HP | × Z11 |
|---|---|---|
| 11 | 4.14 B | 1.0 |
| 12 | 15.88 B | 3.8 |
| 13 | 39.10 B | 9.4 |
| 14 | 69.73 B | 16.8 |
| 15 | 166.05 B | 40.1 |

Z11 is beatable — Legendary cryo clears it at ~368 s, giving **11.25 M/s**. A
mixed two-element battery against a phase boss keeps roughly
`0.5 + 0.5 × 0.15 = 0.575` of that, so **≈ 6.5 M/s**. At that rate:

| Zone | TTK with Z11-era exotics |
|---|---|
| 12 | 41 min |
| 13 | 100 min |
| 14 | 3.0 h |
| 15 | 7.1 h |

The classification test from the skill is "the numbers required to fix it by
tuning are absurd". Making Z15 a 400 s fight by cutting HP means cutting it
**40×**, which puts the corrosion-loop capstone *below Zone 11*. Every tuning
option makes something else worse. That is a missing system, not a wrong number.

**The HP curve is not the bug.** Working backwards from the HP ladder to the
weapon values needed for a ~400 s fight produces a per-zone step of ×2.5, ×1.8,
×2.4 — averaging ×2.2, which is exactly the conventional ladder's clean-Common
tier ratio. The HP curve is internally consistent with a weapon ladder that was
never built.

### The ruling

Ship one exotic weapon per element per NG+ zone — 8 modules — mirroring how
every conventional zone gets its own weapon tier.

| Zone | Cryo variant | Corrosion variant |
|---|---|---|
| 12 | 60,000 | 72,000 |
| 13 | 150,000 | 180,000 |
| 14 | 270,000 | 324,000 |
| 15 | 640,000 | 768,000 |

Derived from `HP / 400 s` at the measured 0.575 phase-mix factor, holding the
existing corrosion:cryo ratio of 1.2. The uneven steps are correct — they track
the actual HP curve, including Z14 being only 1.78× Z13.

Each should follow the conventional pattern: craftable, gated behind that zone's
research node, cost obeying `COST_MIN_DIRECT_DEPTH` (depth 6 at these tiers),
with the zone boss as an alternative drop source.

### Declined

- **Cutting boss HP.** Would invert the NG+ ladder and make the capstone easier
  than the zone that introduces the mechanic.
- **Raising `phase_cut` above 0.15.** That weakens the phase gate itself, which
  is the only interesting thing about these fights — it would turn a loadout
  puzzle back into a stat check.
- **Buffing `cryo_lance` in place.** It is the tuning anchor for Z11 and Z12,
  both of which are correctly tuned against its current value. Moving it breaks
  a working gate to patch a missing one.

### Guard

`boss_gearcheck` already covers it: Z12–Z15 go green when the ladder ships. Until
then the suite correctly reports **4 violations**, and that number is the
tracking metric for this work.

## Note on reading numbers in this codebase

`_apply_enemy_tier_rebase()` runs once in `combat_manager._init()` and multiplies
every enemy's authored stats by `tier_rebase(zone)` — about **×121.5 at Zone 10**.
The values written in the `enemy_db` literal are therefore **pre-rebase** and are
not what ships. Always quote the post-rebase number (what the probes print), and
say which one you mean. Confusing the two produced a wrong finding during the
audit.

## Addendum — the ladder shipped, and it exposed the other half

The eight weapons are in (`z12`–`z15` × Lance/Etcher), gated by four new research
techs, dropped by their own zone's boss. But the spec's authored values were
**far too high**, and fixing that revealed that offence was never the whole
problem.

### The authored values were wrong by 3–5×

`generate_module_drop` scales a module's stats by its `zone`, so the same
authored number is worth much more at Z15 than at Z12 — a Legendary `cryo_lance`
with 10,000 authored `atk_cryo` fights at **3.19 M** per shot. The spec solved
`HP / 400 s` from raw authored numbers and ignored that chain entirely.

Measured by actually killing each boss with survivability neutralised:

| Zone | Spec value | Measured TTK | Corrected | TTK after |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 12 | 60 K / 72 K | 291 s | unchanged | **267 s** |
| 13 | 150 K / 180 K | 132 s | 70 K / 84 K | **312 s** |
| 14 | 270 K / 324 K | 83 s | 81 K / 97 K | **345 s** |
| 15 | 640 K / 768 K | 53 s | 122 K / 146 K | not measurable — see below |

Z12–Z14 now sit in a 267–345 s band, right where a capstone belongs.

### The real wall is defence, and it is not close

With the corrected weapons equipped, measured survival against each NG+ boss on
the best available defence:

| Zone | Survives | Needs | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| 11 | 154 s | 341 s | 2.2× |
| 12 | 13 s | 267 s | 20× |
| 13 | 7 s | 312 s | 45× |
| 14 | 8 s | 345 s | 43× |
| 15 | 5 s | — | boss one-shots a 36 M hull |

The cause is the same shape as the weapon gap: **NG+ has no armour or shield of
its own.** `boss_gearcheck` clamps defence to `z10_armor` / `z10_shield` because
nothing better exists, so the player's defensive power is frozen at tier 10
while these bosses' attack scales four sectors past it. Z15 lands single hits
larger than a fully-geared hull pool, which is why its TTK cannot even be
measured — the keep-alive cannot outrun a one-shot.

`boss_gearcheck` therefore still reports Z12–Z15 as violations, correctly. The
weapons are no longer the reason.

### Next: an NG+ defensive ladder

Same shape as the weapon fix — `z12`–`z15` armour and shield, gated on the same
research, dropped by the same bosses — sized so survival reaches roughly 1.5× the
measured TTK at each zone. Do **not** close the gap by inflating weapon damage:
the 267–345 s band is now correct, and buying survivability with faster kills
would collapse these fights back into stat checks.

### Also noted

Z11's gear-check verdict rides on one marginal cell — the Zone-10 Unique row has
come in at 8/9, 6/9 and 4/9 across three runs against a 60% pass bar, so the
zone flips between OK and FAIL on variance alone. That cell needs more trials or
a wider band before its verdict means anything.

## Addendum 2 — closed: 15 of 15

The defensive ladder shipped alongside the weapons (`z12`–`z15` armour and
shield, same four research techs, `power_tier` 9). It took two corrections and
one genuine boss-tuning finding to land.

**The first pass made everyone immortal.** Consumable heals are percentage-based,
so a bigger pool heals faster; past a threshold, sustain simply outruns incoming
damage and the fight can never be lost. `boss_gearcheck` went from "Common loses
0/9" straight to "Common WINS 9/9" — the pendulum, not a near-miss. Cut the pool
to ×0.65 and the shield regen by 10× (stacking a 5%-of-max regen on top of
percentage heals was the compounding part).

**Z15's real problem was not defence at all.** Its authored ATK was **152 K — the
lowest of the four NG+ bosses**, against Z12's 256 K. Nobody ever died to the
corrosion capstone, so it degenerated into a pure damage race that Common could
finish inside the cap. The proof: cutting Z15's defence by 40% changed the result
by *zero seconds*. Raised to 304 K, which also puts the capstone above the sector
that introduces the mechanic.

**Z12 was the mirror image** — highest ATK of the four and Legendary cleared only
1/9. Lowered to 205 K.

Final matrix, all fifteen bosses honouring the rule:

| Zone | Common | Uncommon | Rare | Legendary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 12 | 0/9 (88% left) | 0/9 (82%) | 0/9 (27%) | **6/9 W246 s** |
| 13 | 0/9 (82%) | 0/9 (71%) | 6/9 W381 s | **9/9 W312 s** |
| 14 | 0/9 (61%) | 0/9 (31%) | 8/9 W352 s | **9/9 W281 s** |
| 15 | 0/9 (53%) | 0/9 (17%) | 8/9 W314 s | **9/9 W261 s** |

Kills land at 246–381 s and the near-miss gradient is there at every zone —
Uncommon at Z15 gets the capstone to 17% remaining, which is exactly the
"one more tier and you have it" signal the rule exists to produce.

### Still open

- **Z11 passes only through the Zone-10 Unique clause**, and that cell has read
  4/9, 5/9, 6/9, 7/9 and 8/9 across runs against a 60% bar. Its Rare and
  Legendary rows both sit at 0/9. The zone's verdict is decided by a coin flip;
  it needs more trials or its own tuning pass.
- **`zone_gate_check` still reports 9 violating cells** (maxed zone-N gear can
  farm zone N+1 trash). Untouched here — it needs the design ruling on whether
  that slow-farm bootstrap is intended.

## Ruling 3 — Z11 must clear on Rare gear from its own sector (owner ruling)

**Status: SHIPPED.** Z11 clears on **Rare 16/21 (76%)**, Legendary 17/21 (81%),
measured at 21 trials.

Z11 was passing only through the Zone-10 Unique clause — a carried ×2.4–3.2
rarity roll papering over a missing tier. Owner ruling: the zone must be
clearable with Rare or better **from that zone**.

### What it took, and the two wrong turns

Z11 was the one sector with **no gear set of its own**: the Threshold handed you
a Cryo-Lance and nothing to wear, so defence fell back to `z10_armor`/`z10_shield`.
Added a `z11_armor` / `z11_shield` pair on `cryo_armaments`, and doubled
`cryo_lance` 10 K → 20 K, since every other sector's boss is answered by that
sector's own weapon tier while Z11's was the entry-level prestige unlock.

Two things went wrong on the way, both worth recording:

**Nine trials could not resolve this.** Against a 60% pass bar, Z11's Rare and
Legendary rows swung 0/9 → 2/9 → 6/9 → 3/9 across *identical* settings, and a
1.3× kit buff appeared to make Legendary worse. I tuned against that noise for
several rounds before running at 21 trials, which showed the true rates were 24%
and 57% — genuinely under the bar, not unlucky. **Re-read the "high variance
means the metric is unstable" note in the skill before tuning anything near a
threshold.**

**Compensating a hard boss with strong gear inverted the ladder.** Getting Rare
over the bar by kit alone required authoring the Z11 modules at 2.6× the Z12
ones, which would have made a Z11 drop beat a Z12 drop. Reverted, put the Z11 kit
one honest step below Z12 (def 24,000 vs 22,820 authored, which after ~1.45×/zone
scaling leaves Z12 clearly stronger), and took the difference out of the boss
instead: ATK 280 K → 105 K, HP 20 M → 15 M. Z11 had been hitting *harder* than
Z12 (280 K vs 205 K) — the warp arc was inverted at its own entry point.

### Final matrix, 21 trials

| Zone | Common | Uncommon | Rare | Legendary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 11 | 0/21 (91% left) | 0/21 (86%) | **16/21 W161 s** | 17/21 W136 s |
| 12 | 0/21 (68%) | 0/21 (50%) | 17/21 W268 s | 19/21 W219 s |
| 13 | 0/21 (82%) | 0/21 (71%) | 15/21 W375 s | 17/21 W306 s |
| 14 | 0/21 (59%) | 0/21 (31%) | 20/21 W355 s | 20/21 W280 s |
| 15 | 0/21 (52%) | 0/21 (18%) | 18/21 W312 s | 21/21 W263 s |

Fifteen of fifteen bosses honour the rule, no zone passes on borrowed gear, and
Common/Uncommon lose everywhere with a visible near-miss gradient.
