# Design rulings — 2026-08-08

Two open gear-check violations from the [full audit](audit/AUDIT_2026-08-08.md),
ruled using the procedure in `.claude/skills/rpg-game-design`: measure, distrust
the instrument, classify, rule with a number, guard it.

---

## Ruling 1 — Z10 Void Leviathan: ATK 53,333 → 64,000 (×1.20)

**Status: SHIPPED.** `combat_manager.gd`, `z10_boss_leviathan.stats.atk`.

### The finding

`boss_gearcheck` rule is *Common and Uncommon must LOSE, Rare and above must
WIN*. Z10 failed it — tier-matched Uncommon cleared the capstone **9/9 at
~263 s**.

### What the measurement said

Built `scripts/sim/z10_gate_diag.gd` to find which axis the gate turns on,
because that decides the lever:

| Rarity | Result (before) |
|---|---|
| Common | 0/9, **died** at 185 s, boss 40% left |
| Uncommon | 9/9, 263 s, **zero deaths in 9** |
| Rare | 9/9, 177 s |
| Legendary | 9/9, 143 s |

The gate turns on **survival, not damage**. Common dies; Uncommon never does.
That immediately rules out the obvious fix: Uncommon does not time out, so
raising HP cannot stop it. Enough HP to push Uncommon past the 1500 s cap
(~5.7×) would drag Rare to ~1000 s and Legendary to ~815 s — three times longer
than a boss should run.

An ATK sweep showed Rare is **unmoved across the entire band** — 176 s at ×1.0,
188 s at ×2.5, which is noise. Uncommon is a knife-edge: 9/9 in one run and 3/5
in another at ×1.0, then 0/5 by ×1.3. So there is a wide safe corridor to spend
and only Uncommon is sensitive to it.

First attempt at ×1.40 overshot: Uncommon died at 72 s with **76% boss HP left**.
That satisfies the rule but reads as "you are not welcome here" rather than
"you are nearly ready", and a gate with no near-miss row teaches nothing.

### The ruling

**×1.20.** Measured clean:

| Rarity | Result (after) |
|---|---|
| Common | 0/9, dies 105 s, boss 66% left |
| Uncommon | 0/9, dies 145 s, boss **46% left** |
| Rare | 9/9, **218 s** |
| Legendary | 9/9, **152 s** |

Uncommon now takes the boss past halfway before dying — visible progress,
obviously nearly possible, come back with one more tier. Rare and Legendary sit
inside the 90–300 s band a boss should occupy. There is 20% margin over the ×1.0
knife-edge, so the guard will not flip red on variance.

### Declined

- **Raising HP.** Wrong lever (Uncommon never times out) and it would wreck
  Rare/Legendary TTK.
- **Touching the rarity curve.** It is a shared constant that forty other
  encounters depend on; one boss's problem does not justify moving it.
- **×1.10**, which gave the prettiest near-miss (21% boss HP left) but sits only
  10% above the knife-edge — too thin for a stable guard.

### Guard

`scenes/boss_gearcheck.tscn` — Z10 now reports OK, and the suite is **11 of 15**
honoring the rule (was 10). `scenes/z10_gate_diag.tscn` is kept as a diagnostic
(no assertions; it reports the matrix) for the next question of this shape.

## Ruling 2 — Z12–Z15: the exotic weapon ladder is missing. Do not tune this.

**Status: SPEC. Not a tuning change — content work.**

### The finding

All four NG+ Loop 1 bosses lose **0/9 at every rarity including Legendary**,
even with the correct mixed cryo+corrosion battery that their phase gate demands.

### Why this is a design gap, not a balance problem

The conventional ladder ships **30 weapons across 10 zones** — a fresh tier
every zone. The exotic channel ships **two weapons for five zones**:

| Module | atk_cryo | Era |
|---|---|---|
| `cryo_lance` | 10,000 | Z11 (first-warp unlock) |
| `corrosion_blaster` | 12,000 | Z11/Z12 |

Those two are the *only* things that breach a phase band. So across Z11 → Z15
the player's conventional power scales normally while their **exotic power is
frozen at tier 11** — and exotic power is the only kind that counts there.

Meanwhile boss HP keeps climbing:

| Zone | Effective HP | × Z11 |
|---|---|---|
| 11 | 4.14 B | 1.0 |
| 12 | 15.88 B | 3.8 |
| 13 | 39.10 B | 9.4 |
| 14 | 69.73 B | 16.8 |
| 15 | 166.05 B | 40.1 |

Z11 is beatable — Legendary cryo clears it at ~368 s, giving **11.25 M/s**. A
mixed two-element battery against a phase boss keeps roughly
`0.5 + 0.5 × 0.15 = 0.575` of that, so **≈ 6.5 M/s**. At that rate:

| Zone | TTK with Z11-era exotics |
|---|---|
| 12 | 41 min |
| 13 | 100 min |
| 14 | 3.0 h |
| 15 | 7.1 h |

The classification test from the skill is "the numbers required to fix it by
tuning are absurd". Making Z15 a 400 s fight by cutting HP means cutting it
**40×**, which puts the corrosion-loop capstone *below Zone 11*. Every tuning
option makes something else worse. That is a missing system, not a wrong number.

**The HP curve is not the bug.** Working backwards from the HP ladder to the
weapon values needed for a ~400 s fight produces a per-zone step of ×2.5, ×1.8,
×2.4 — averaging ×2.2, which is exactly the conventional ladder's clean-Common
tier ratio. The HP curve is internally consistent with a weapon ladder that was
never built.

### The ruling

Ship one exotic weapon per element per NG+ zone — 8 modules — mirroring how
every conventional zone gets its own weapon tier.

| Zone | Cryo variant | Corrosion variant |
|---|---|---|
| 12 | 60,000 | 72,000 |
| 13 | 150,000 | 180,000 |
| 14 | 270,000 | 324,000 |
| 15 | 640,000 | 768,000 |

Derived from `HP / 400 s` at the measured 0.575 phase-mix factor, holding the
existing corrosion:cryo ratio of 1.2. The uneven steps are correct — they track
the actual HP curve, including Z14 being only 1.78× Z13.

Each should follow the conventional pattern: craftable, gated behind that zone's
research node, cost obeying `COST_MIN_DIRECT_DEPTH` (depth 6 at these tiers),
with the zone boss as an alternative drop source.

### Declined

- **Cutting boss HP.** Would invert the NG+ ladder and make the capstone easier
  than the zone that introduces the mechanic.
- **Raising `phase_cut` above 0.15.** That weakens the phase gate itself, which
  is the only interesting thing about these fights — it would turn a loadout
  puzzle back into a stat check.
- **Buffing `cryo_lance` in place.** It is the tuning anchor for Z11 and Z12,
  both of which are correctly tuned against its current value. Moving it breaks
  a working gate to patch a missing one.

### Guard

`boss_gearcheck` already covers it: Z12–Z15 go green when the ladder ships. Until
then the suite correctly reports **4 violations**, and that number is the
tracking metric for this work.

## Note on reading numbers in this codebase

`_apply_enemy_tier_rebase()` runs once in `combat_manager._init()` and multiplies
every enemy's authored stats by `tier_rebase(zone)` — about **×121.5 at Zone 10**.
The values written in the `enemy_db` literal are therefore **pre-rebase** and are
not what ships. Always quote the post-rebase number (what the probes print), and
say which one you mean. Confusing the two produced a wrong finding during the
audit.
