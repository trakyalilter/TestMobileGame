My read: Line keeps nav quiet and utilitarian (icons shouldn't shout louder than the active-state highlight). Solid has the most presence and pairs well with a content area full of detailed item tiles. Duotone is the richest and most on-brand for the console aesthetic, but the most work to keep clean at 19px. For a sidebar I lean Line or Solid; Duotone if you want max polish.
