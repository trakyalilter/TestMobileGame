Enemy Card — Stat Icons

Replacing the unicode glyphs (♥ HP · ⚔ ATK · ⛨ DEF) with custom SVGs. All ship as white shapes tinted per-stat via modulate — same system as the rest of the icon set. Shown below at the real card size (15px) plus a detail strip. I included DEF too — leaving one emoji among two custom icons would look broken.

My pick: A (Solid) for the 15px in-card size — filled shapes stay crisp and instantly readable where thin strokes turn to mush. C (Space-Tech) is the characterful option (vitals-pulse heart, energy blades, hex deflector) and fits the fantasy — best if you want the cards to feel less generic-RPG. Easy hybrid: A's heart+swords for legibility, C's hex deflector for DEF. B (line) matches the module-icon family but is the weakest at this tiny size.
